# POLARCONNECT — 3 to 5 Minute Demo Script

## 1. Opening
“POLARCONNECT is a remote monitoring and predictive decision-support platform for Antarctic research stations. The main goal is to reduce maintenance workload so scientists can spend more time on research.”

## 2. Show healthy station
Open the main dashboard.

Explain:
- fuel;
- electrical demand;
- water;
- food;
- indoor/outdoor temperature;
- population.

Point to the message that all monitored thresholds are within range.

## 3. Show 3D station
Explain that major systems are mapped to physical zones.

Mention:
- laboratories;
- living/heating;
- power;
- water treatment;
- fuel;
- cold storage;
- distribution pipe.

## 4. Create a scenario
In Simulation Lab, choose a change such as:
- lower water output;
- lower generation;
- worn pipe;
- colder weather;
- or more people.

Say:
“Instead of waiting for a real failure, we can test the effect first.”

## 5. Run the forecast
Choose the future duration and run the simulation.

Explain:
“The engine recalculates demand, capacity and resource levels across the future time window.”

## 6. Show predicted risk
Open Forecast Explanations.

Say:
“POLARCONNECT shows not only the alert, but the first crossing time, the calculated reason and the affected resource/system.”

## 7. Show red zone
Click `Show time & red zone`.

Say:
“The numerical prediction is linked to the physical zone in the 3D station, making the problem easier to understand.”

## 8. Compare plans
Show Plan A and Plan B.

Explain:
- Plan A reduces demand;
- Plan B restores capacity;
- the system recalculates both;
- operator decides which action is appropriate.

## 9. Research impact
Open Population & Research Capacity.

Say:
“The purpose is not to remove people blindly. It is to reduce avoidable monitoring and maintenance workload and show how resource decisions affect the station’s ability to support research.”

## 10. Closing
“POLARCONNECT combines monitoring, simulation, predictive alerts, root-cause explanation, 3D visualization and response comparison in one platform. With live sensor and weather inputs, the forecast would continuously update instead of remaining a fixed prediction.”
